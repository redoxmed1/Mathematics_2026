### Exercise 10. Triangle Area from Vectors

**Problem**

The points

A = (0, 0, 0)

B = (2, 1, 0)

C = (1, 3, 2)

form a triangle.

1. Construct the vectors AB and AC using the coordinates of the points.
2. Compute the cross product AB × AC.
3. Use the length of the cross product to calculate the area of the parallelogram formed by the two vectors.
4. Find the area of the triangle.
5. Explain why the factor 1/2 is needed.

**Solution**

**Step 1. Construct vector AB**

To construct a vector between two points, subtract the coordinates of the starting point from the coordinates of the ending point.

AB = B - A

AB = (2 - 0, 1 - 0, 0 - 0)

Therefore:

AB = (2, 1, 0)

**Step 2. Construct vector AC**

AC = C - A

AC = (1 - 0, 3 - 0, 2 - 0)

Therefore:

AC = (1, 3, 2)

**Step 3. Calculate the cross product**

The cross product is:

AB × AC =

| i    j    k |
| 2    1    0 |
| 1    3    2 |

Calculate each component:

First component:

1(2) - 0(3) = 2

Second component:

0(1) - 2(2) = -4

Third component:

2(3) - 1(1) = 5

Therefore:

AB × AC = (2, -4, 5)

**Step 4. Calculate the parallelogram area**

The area of a parallelogram formed by two vectors is the length of their cross product.

Area = ||AB × AC||

Substitute:

Area = √(2² + (-4)² + 5²)

Area = √(4 + 16 + 25)

Area = √45

Area = 3√5

Therefore, the parallelogram area is:

3√5

**Step 5. Calculate the triangle area**

A triangle formed by two sides of a parallelogram occupies exactly half of the parallelogram's area.

Therefore:

Triangle Area = (1/2) × Parallelogram Area

Triangle Area = (1/2) × 3√5

Triangle Area = 3√5 / 2

**Step 6. Explain the factor 1/2**

The two vectors AB and AC form two adjacent sides of a parallelogram.

The diagonal of the parallelogram divides it into two equal triangles.

Therefore, the area of one triangle is half the area of the parallelogram.

**Final Answer**

AB = (2, 1, 0)

AC = (1, 3, 2)

AB × AC = (2, -4, 5)

Parallelogram area = 3√5

Triangle area = 3√5 / 2
