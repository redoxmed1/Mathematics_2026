### Exercise 9. Distances

**Problem**

Compute:

1. The distance from $P=(2,3)$ to the line $3x+4y-10=0$.
2. The distance from $Q=(2,-1,3)$ to the plane $2x-2y+z-4=0$.

Compare the two formulas and explain their common connection with the normal direction.

**Solution**

**1. Distance from a point to a line**

The distance from a point $(x_0,y_0)$ to a line $ax+by+c=0$ is:

$$
d=
\frac{|ax_0+by_0+c|}
{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}
$$

Given:

$$
P=(2,3)
$$

$$
3x+4y-10=0
$$

Therefore:

$$
a=3,\quad b=4,\quad c=-10
$$

Substituting:

$$
d=
\frac{|3(2)+4(3)-10|}
{\sqrt{3^2+4^2}}
$$

$$
d=\frac{|6+12-10|}{\sqrt{9+16}}
$$

$$
d=\frac8{5}
$$

**2. Distance from a point to a plane**

The distance from a point $(x_0,y_0,z_0)$ to a plane $ax+by+cz+d=0$ is:

$$
d=
\frac{|ax_0+by_0+cz_0+d|}
{\sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2}}
$$

Given:

$$
Q=(2,-1,3)
$$

$$
2x-2y+z-4=0
$$

Therefore:

$$
a=2,\quad b=-2,\quad c=1,\quad d=-4
$$

Substituting:

$$
d=
\frac{|2(2)-2(-1)+3-4|}
{\sqrt{2^2+(-2)^2+1^2}}
$$

$$
d=\frac{|4+2+3-4|}{\sqrt{4+4+1}}
$$

$$
d=\frac5{3}
$$

**3. Comparing the formulas**

The distance from the point to the line is:

$$
d_1=\frac85
$$

The distance from the point to the plane is:

$$
d_2=\frac53
$$

Both formulas use the same geometric principle.

The shortest distance is measured in the direction perpendicular to the line or plane.

The coefficients of the equations form their normal vectors.

For the line, the normal vector is:

$$
n_1=(3,4)
$$

For the plane, the normal vector is:

$$
n_2=(2,-2,1)
$$

The denominators of both formulas are the lengths of their corresponding normal vectors.

**Final Results**

Distance from $P$ to the line:

$$
d_1=\frac85
$$

Distance from $Q$ to the plane:

$$
d_2=\frac53
$$
