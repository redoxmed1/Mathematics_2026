### Exercise 1. Recognizing Curves from Canonical Form

**Problem**

Recognize the curves:

1. x² + y² = 9

2. x²/16 + y²/9 = 1

3. y² = 8x

4. x²/9 - y²/4 = 1

For each one, identify the most important features and make a sketch. Explain which parts of the equation let you recognize the type of curve.

**Solution**

**1. First curve:**

x² + y² = 9

This is the canonical equation of a circle:

x² + y² = r²

Therefore:

- Center: C = (0, 0)
- Radius: r = 3
- The circle is centered at the origin.

Some points on the circle are:

(3, 0), (-3, 0), (0, 3), (0, -3)

**2. Second curve:**

x²/16 + y²/9 = 1

This is the canonical equation of an ellipse:

x²/a² + y²/b² = 1

Comparing the equations:

a² = 16, so a = 4

b² = 9, so b = 3

Therefore:

- Center: C = (0, 0)
- Horizontal semiaxis: 4
- Vertical semiaxis: 3
- The ellipse is stretched more along the x-axis.

The vertices are:

(4, 0), (-4, 0), (0, 3), (0, -3)

**3. Third curve:**

y² = 8x

The canonical equation of a parabola opening to the right is:

y² = 4px

Comparing:

4p = 8

p = 2

Therefore:

- Vertex: V = (0, 0)
- Axis of symmetry: x-axis
- Opening direction: right
- Focus: F = (2, 0)
- Directrix: x = -2

**4. Fourth curve:**

x²/9 - y²/4 = 1

This is the canonical equation of a hyperbola:

x²/a² - y²/b² = 1

Comparing:

a² = 9, so a = 3

b² = 4, so b = 2

Therefore:

- Center: C = (0, 0)
- Vertices: (3, 0) and (-3, 0)
- The branches open to the left and right.

The asymptotes are:

y = (2/3)x

y = -(2/3)x

**Conclusion**

We recognize the curves by their equations:

- Circle: x² + y² = r²
- Ellipse: x²/a² + y²/b² = 1
- Parabola: y² = 4px
- Hyperbola: x²/a² - y²/b² = 1
