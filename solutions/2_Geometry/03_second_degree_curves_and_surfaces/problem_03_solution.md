### Exercise 3. Ellipse

**Problem**

For

x²/25 + y²/9 = 1

find the semiaxis lengths, the intercepts with the coordinate axes, and make a sketch. Explain along which axis the ellipse is more stretched and how this is visible in the equation.

**Solution**

**Step 1. Identify the canonical form**

The canonical equation of an ellipse is:

x²/a² + y²/b² = 1

Comparing with the given equation:

x²/25 + y²/9 = 1

We obtain:

a² = 25

b² = 9

Therefore:

a = 5

b = 3

**Step 2. Find the center**

The center is:

C = (0, 0)

**Step 3. Find the semiaxis lengths**

- Horizontal semiaxis: a = 5
- Vertical semiaxis: b = 3

**Step 4. Find the intercepts with the x-axis**

On the x-axis:

y = 0

Substitute:

x²/25 = 1

x² = 25

x = ±5

The intercepts are:

(5, 0) and (-5, 0)

**Step 5. Find the intercepts with the y-axis**

On the y-axis:

x = 0

Substitute:

y²/9 = 1

y² = 9

y = ±3

The intercepts are:

(0, 3) and (0, -3)

**Step 6. Describe the ellipse**

The ellipse is centered at the origin.

It is more stretched along the x-axis because:

5 > 3

The horizontal semiaxis is longer than the vertical semiaxis.

**Final Answer**

- Center: C = (0, 0)
- Horizontal semiaxis: 5
- Vertical semiaxis: 3
- x-intercepts: (5, 0), (-5, 0)
- y-intercepts: (0, 3), (0, -3)

The ellipse is wider horizontally than vertically.
