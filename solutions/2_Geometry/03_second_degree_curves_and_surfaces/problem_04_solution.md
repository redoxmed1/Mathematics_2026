### Exercise 4. Parabola

**Problem**

For

y² = 12x

identify the vertex, axis of symmetry, and opening direction. Find points on the parabola for x = 3 and use them to make a sketch.

**Solution**

**Step 1. Identify the canonical form**

The canonical equation of a parabola opening to the right is:

y² = 4px

Given:

y² = 12x

Comparing:

4p = 12

p = 3

**Step 2. Find the vertex**

The vertex is:

V = (0, 0)

**Step 3. Find the axis of symmetry**

The equation contains y² and x.

Therefore, the axis of symmetry is:

y = 0

This is the x-axis.

**Step 4. Find the opening direction**

Since the coefficient of x is positive, the parabola opens to the right.

**Step 5. Find the points for x = 3**

Substitute x = 3:

y² = 12(3)

y² = 36

y = ±6

Therefore, the two points are:

P = (3, 6)

Q = (3, -6)

**Step 6. Describe the parabola**

The parabola has:

- Vertex: V = (0, 0)
- Axis of symmetry: y = 0
- Opening direction: right
- Focus: F = (3, 0)
- Directrix: x = -3

The points (3, 6) and (3, -6) can be used to draw the sketch.

**Final Answer**

The parabola opens to the right, has its vertex at the origin, and is symmetric about the x-axis.
