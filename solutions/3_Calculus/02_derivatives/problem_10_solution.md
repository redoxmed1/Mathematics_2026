# Exercise 10: The Tangent Line as an Application of the Derivative

## Problem

For

$$
f(x)=x^2-3x+1
$$

find the equation of the tangent line at the point with $x=2$.

Give the point of tangency, the value $f'(2)$, the equation of the line, and verify that the point lies on the obtained tangent line.

## Solution

### Step 1: Find the point of tangency

The point of tangency has an $x$-coordinate equal to 2.

To find its $y$-coordinate, calculate $f(2)$:

$$
f(2)=2^2-3(2)+1.
$$

First, calculate the power:

$$
2^2=4.
$$

Then calculate the multiplication:

$$
3(2)=6.
$$

Substitute:

$$
f(2)=4-6+1.
$$

$$
f(2)=-2+1=-1.
$$

Therefore, the point of tangency is

$$
\boxed{P=(2,-1)}
$$

### Step 2: Calculate the derivative

The original function is

$$
f(x)=x^2-3x+1.
$$

Using the power rule and the constant rule:

$$
f'(x)=2x-3.
$$

Therefore,

$$
\boxed{f'(x)=2x-3}
$$

### Step 3: Calculate the slope at $x=2$

Substitute $x=2$ into the derivative:

$$
f'(2)=2(2)-3.
$$

$$
f'(2)=4-3.
$$

Therefore,

$$
\boxed{f'(2)=1}
$$

The slope of the tangent line is 1.

### Step 4: Find the equation of the tangent line

The point-slope equation of a line is

$$
y-y_0=m(x-x_0),
$$

where:

- $(x_0,y_0)$ is the point on the line.
- $m$ is the slope.

We know that

$$
(x_0,y_0)=(2,-1)
$$

and

$$
m=1.
$$

Substitute these values:

$$
y-(-1)=1(x-2).
$$

Simplify:

$$
y+1=x-2.
$$

Subtract 1 from both sides:

$$
y=x-2-1.
$$

Therefore,

$$
\boxed{y=x-3}
$$

### Step 5: Verify that the point lies on the tangent line

The point of tangency is

$$
P=(2,-1).
$$

The tangent line is

$$
y=x-3.
$$

Substitute $x=2$ into the line equation:

$$
y=2-3.
$$

$$
y=-1.
$$

This gives the point

$$
(2,-1),
$$

which is exactly the point of tangency.

Therefore, the point lies on the tangent line.

## Final Answer

Point of tangency:

$$
\boxed{P=(2,-1)}
$$

Derivative:

$$
\boxed{f'(x)=2x-3}
$$

Slope at $x=2$:

$$
\boxed{f'(2)=1}
$$

Equation of the tangent line:

$$
\boxed{y=x-3}
$$
